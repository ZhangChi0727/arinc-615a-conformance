## Meaning of "candidate"

v4.3 is a candidate certification-oriented evidence-architecture baseline.
Until a reviewed gate approves it and the PR merges, `RB-2026-001-v4.2.1`
remains the effective and frozen repository baseline. v4.3 does not retroactively
alter previous evidence and does not claim certification credit.

## Core semantic change

This baseline separates three previously interleaved concerns:

1. certification-oriented assurance;
2. protocol-conformance engineering;
3. research evaluation.

It introduces a seven-layer evidence model (L0–L7) and superseds the single
T0–T3 ladder with two orthogonal axes:

- certification-oriented assurance states A0–A4;
- research-evidence maturity states R0–R5.

`A4` does not depend on `R4` or `R5`.

## Status semantics

Execution validity, case verdict, objective status, and compliance status are
distinct. A single `PASS` never automatically promotes a requirement or
compliance claim.

## Migration rules

1. `RB-2026-001-v4.2.1` remains frozen.
2. v4.3 does not retroactively alter previous evidence.
3. historical T0–T3 labels are not silently converted.
4. existing raw evidence may be referenced by v4.3 only with explicit
   re-evaluation under the new objective and closure contracts.
5. timing formulas and robust timing semantics remain unchanged.
6. no existing empirical claim is promoted by the restructuring alone.
7. no certification credit is gained by adopting the new architecture.
8. new A0–A4 states are project-defined assurance states.

## Required non-claims

- certification-oriented does not mean certification-approved;
- internal assurance gates are not authority review gates;
- protocol conformance does not establish aircraft-level airworthiness
  compliance;
- mutation adequacy is not a certification objective;
- Bayesian or statistical calibration is not required to establish ordinary
  protocol verification evidence;
- tool qualification credit is not claimed unless explicitly established under
  an applicable qualification basis;
- aircraft and system-level certification requirements are not fabricated in the
  protocol-only study.

## “候选”的含义

v4.3 是候选的面向认证证据架构基线。在受评审门禁批准且 PR 合并前，`RB-2026-001-v4.2.1` 仍为已生效并冻结的仓库基线。v4.3 不回溯修改既有证据，也不主张认证信用。

## 核心语义变化

本基线分离此前交织的三个关注点：面向认证的保证、协议符合性工程和研究评价。它引入七层证据模型（L0–L7），并以两个正交轴替代单一 T0–T3 阶梯：面向认证保证状态 A0–A4；研究证据成熟度状态 R0–R5。`A4` 不依赖 `R4` 或 `R5`。

## 状态语义

执行有效性、用例判定、目标状态与合规状态相互区别。单个 `PASS` 不得自动晋级需求或合规主张。

## 迁移规则

RB-2026-001-v4.2.1 保持冻结；v4.3 不回溯修改既有证据；历史 T0–T3 标签不静默转换；既有原始证据仅在新的目标与关闭契约下显式重评后可被 v4.3 引用；时序公式与稳健时序语义不变；重构本身不晋级任何既有经验主张；采用新架构不获得认证信用；A0–A4 为项目自定义保证状态。

## 必需非主张

面向认证不等于已获认证；内部保证门不是权威评审门；协议符合性不确立航空器级适航合规；突变充分性不是认证目标；贝叶斯或统计校准非建立普通协议验证证据所必需；除非在适用鉴定基础上明确建立，否则不主张工具鉴定信用；仅协议研究不编造航空器与系统级认证需求。
