## Decision and boundaries

Use one machine-readable package as the sole authority for M1 source bindings,
scope, coverage, applicability, atomic CRS items, dependencies, gaps, review
metadata, fingerprints and activation. The Markdown view is generated and owns
no state. ARINC 615A-3 receives a complete coverage audit; ARINC 665-5 receives
only a dependency-bounded survey triggered by the active authority and selected
services. Source modality and conformance effect remain separate: source
`SHOULD` is not downgraded to advice, and implemented `MAY` behavior is split
from its conditional constraints.

This change does not modify `src/`, create an EFSM, test purpose, case,
procedure, Project Configuration, execution evidence, baseline or tag, and does
not establish conformance, certification readiness or authority acceptance.
Existing code and public summaries cannot close an external normative source.

## Conditional transition

- Pre-activation: M0 is externally verified; the M1 gate is `NOT YET ESTABLISHED`.
- Draft candidate: M1 disposition is `ADOPT`; RG0 and RG1 are `PENDING-EXTERNAL-INDEPENDENT-REVIEW`.
- Activation event: a named independent reviewer approves the unchanged final
  Head, an ordinary two-parent merge is made, and its second parent equals that
  approved Head.
- Post-activation: the package is approved as qualified M2 input, subject to
  every recorded dependency and gap, without establishing conformance.

Repository automation validates structure and repository-side merge evidence;
it does not manufacture independent approval. M2 remains prohibited until the
joint event is externally verified.

## REWORK disposition after first independent review

The first RG0/RG1 review rejected the earlier page-sentence inventory. The
replacement package uses unique leaf source units for prose sentences, table
rows and sequence-chart events. Every CRS item repeats and must exactly match
its coverage source-unit identity, locator, hash, source modality, conformance
effect, applicability and rationale. Its bilingual review semantics expose the
actor, condition, action, object and observable effect rather than a category
template.

Timing provenance is now typed as a fixed source constant, message-carried
parameter, configuration parameter, symbolic source parameter or
non-normative example. A field representation range is not a deadline. The
two fixed constants retained by this candidate are bound only to their actual
Attachment 4 source units; the DLP equation/example tension remains visible for
RG1 adjudication. RFC 768, 791, 1123, 1350, 1785, 2347, 2348 and 2349, ARINC
664 Parts 2 and 3, ARINC 665-5 and ARINC 645 have separate dependency
identities.

`m1_rg0_source_inventory_anchor.json` detects package-only coordinated edits,
including deletion, locator/hash replacement, timing injection and dependency
identity folding. This is not a claim that automation can read or prove a
proprietary standard: completeness, boundary fidelity and semantic fidelity
remain explicit external RG0/RG1 responsibilities. The renewed review scope is
the complete branch range from the M1 base to the final unchanged Head.

## REWORK disposition after complete renewed review

The renewed review found that the first reconstruction still inferred clauses
from body cross-references, lost Appendix namespaces, retained compound prose
and table co-ownership, used several reversed or generic semantic
dispositions, collapsed timing events, and broadcast one Profile-level trigger
set to every bounded 665-5 item. The corrected candidate therefore adds a
controlled section-span manifest, single-owner atomicity metadata, structured
status-table rows and footnote, source-specific timing event families,
requirement-specific 615A-to-665-5 edges, and data-driven semantic regression
assertions for independently identified high-risk propositions.

The repository records a candidate snapshot, not an approval claim. Formal M1
activation remains the conditional joint event defined above. M2 entry requires
external verification that the exact reviewed Head was jointly approved, that
an ordinary merge has that Head as its second parent, and that main CI passed.
No post-merge status commit is required or authorized by this change record.

CI follows a two-stage policy: pull-request-event CI is the feature-branch
gate, and main-branch push CI is the post-merge gate. A feature-branch push run
is not required and must not be reported as having occurred.

## RG0 PASS / RG1 REWORK disposition at `290c7a43...`

The third independent review passed RG0 and retained RG1 as REWORK. The
correction separates the ARINC 615A and ARINC 665 modality vocabularies,
restores negative/non-failure propositions, splits the checksum-option source
unit into its target acknowledgement and loader validation obligations, and
binds timing records to source-stated events and equations. Requirement-level
615A-to-665-5 edges are now optional: Profile-scope admission is not an edge,
and every emitted edge must identify a shared controlled semantic object and
carry an auditable relation rationale.

Every candidate requirement is represented exactly once in the controlled
semantic-assertion manifest. The manifest binds source identity, complete
semantic tuple including polarity, modality/effect, dependencies, timing
relation and any requirement-level edge. This mechanical binding prevents
post-review drift; it does not replace renewed full-scope RG1 human review.

## WO-M1-2026-002 mechanical closures

The later working-tree candidate applies the WO-M1-2026-002 Must items on top
of Head `290c7a43...` without inheriting that Head's RG0 result. RG0 and RG1
remain `PENDING-EXTERNAL-INDEPENDENT-REVIEW` on any new Head. Draft automation
validates structure, page-account completeness, source-register hash/role
bindings, closed profileScope/rhoRA/activation constants, and assertion
coverage. It still does not verify merge topology or manufacture independent
approval; `mergeEvidence` remains `NOT-YET-PRESENT` until an external reviewer
approves an unchanged Head.

Registered RFC 768/791/1123/1350/1785/2347/2348/2349 and ARINC 664 Parts 2 and
3 identities are now open dependencies in the source register, with DD-018
listed among the controlled technical-direction decisions. Every registered
PDF page is accounted by a section span or an explicit exclusion range.

# 中文版

# CR-2026-007——建立 M1 CRS 与适用性数据包

| 字段 | 值 |
|---|---|
| 变更分类 | 影响基线的需求／适用性候选 |
| 候选处置 | ADOPT |
| 唯一权威 | `configs/requirements/arinc_615a3_m1_crs.json` |
| 生成视图 | `docs/control/requirements/ARINC615A3_M1_CRS_REVIEW_VIEW.md` |
| 评审门 | 同一不可变 Head 上的 RG0 范围与 RG1 CRS |
| 外部批准 | NOT AUTOMATED |

## 决定与边界

采用一个机器可读数据包作为 M1 来源绑定、范围、覆盖、适用性、原子 CRS、依赖、缺口、评审元数据、指纹和激活的唯一权威；Markdown 视图仅为生成物，不拥有状态。ARINC 615A-3 做完整覆盖审计；ARINC 665-5 仅做由活动权威和所选服务触发的有边界依赖审计。来源原始模态与符合性效果分离：来源 `SHOULD` 不得降为普通建议；`MAY` 能力与“若实现则必须满足”的条件义务拆分。

本变更不修改 `src/`，不创建 EFSM、测试目的、用例、规程、Project Configuration、执行证据、baseline 或 tag，也不建立符合性、认证准备度或权威接受。既有代码与公开摘要不能关闭外部规范来源。

## 条件转换

- 激活前：M0 已外部核验；M1 门为 `NOT YET ESTABLISHED`。
- Draft 候选：M1 处置为 `ADOPT`；RG0/RG1 为 `PENDING-EXTERNAL-INDEPENDENT-REVIEW`。
- 激活事件：具名独立评审者批准不变最终 Head，随后普通两父合并，且第二父等于该 Head。
- 激活后：数据包在全部登记依赖和缺口约束下成为获批的 M2 输入，但不建立符合性。

自动化只校验结构和仓库侧合并证据，不制造独立批准。联合事件经外部核验前禁止 M2。

## 首轮独立评审后的 REWORK 处置

首轮 RG0/RG1 评审否决了此前的页面—句子清单。替代数据包为正文句子、表格行和序列图事件建立唯一叶级来源单元。每个 CRS 项重复其覆盖来源单元的身份、定位符、散列、来源模态、符合性效果、适用性与理由，并由校验器强制逐项一致。双语评审语义明确列出参与者、条件、行为、对象与可观察效果，不再使用类别模板。

时序溯源现区分固定来源常数、消息携带参数、配置参数、符号来源参数及非规范示例；字段表示范围不得充当截止期。本候选只在 Attachment 4 的真实来源单元上保留两个固定常数，并把 DLP 公式／示例之间的张力保留给 RG1 裁决。RFC 768、791、1123、1350、1785、2347、2348、2349，ARINC 664 第 2、3 部分，ARINC 665-5 与 ARINC 645 均拥有独立依赖身份。

`m1_rg0_source_inventory_anchor.json` 用于发现仅修改数据包的协同篡改，包括删除、定位符／散列替换、任意时序注入及依赖身份折叠。这不声称自动化能够读取或证明专有标准；完整性、边界忠实度与语义忠实度仍明确属于外部 RG0/RG1。新一轮评审范围是从 M1 基点到不再变化的最终 Head 的完整分支范围。

## 完整新一轮评审后的 REWORK 处置

完整复审发现，首次重建仍会由正文交叉引用推断条款、丢失 Appendix 命名空间、保留复合正文与表体双重所有权，并存在若干方向反转或通用化的语义处置；时序事件被折叠，Profile 级触发集合也被广播到全部有界 665-5 条目。修正候选因此新增受控 section-span 清单、单一所有者原子性元数据、结构化状态表行与脚注、来源特定的时序事件族、需求级 615A→665-5 边，以及针对独立评审识别的高风险命题的数据驱动语义回归断言。

仓库只记录候选快照，不声明批准。M1 正式激活仍是上文定义的条件联合事件。M2 入口要求外部核验精确受审 Head 已获联合批准、普通合并的第二父正是该 Head，且 main CI 成功。本变更记录不要求也不授权合并后状态提交。

CI 采用两阶段政策：pull-request 事件 CI 是特性分支门禁，main 分支 push CI 是合并后门禁。不要求特性分支 push run，也不得声称其已经发生。

## `290c7a43...` 上的 RG0 PASS／RG1 REWORK 处置

第三次独立评审通过 RG0，但 RG1 仍为 REWORK。本次修正分离 ARINC 615A 与 ARINC 665 的模态词汇，恢复否定／不失败命题，把 checksum option 来源单元拆分为目标硬件确认义务和加载器验证义务，并将时序记录绑定到来源明确规定的事件与方程。需求级 615A→665-5 边现为可选：Profile 范围准入不构成需求边；每条实际生成的边必须指出共享的受控语义对象，并携带可审计的关系理由。

受控语义断言清单恰好一次覆盖每条候选需求，并绑定来源身份、包含极性的完整语义元组、模态／效果、依赖、时序关系及需求级边。该机械绑定用于防止评审后漂移，不替代重新执行的完整范围 RG1 人工评审。

## WO-M1-2026-002 机械关闭

后续工作树候选在 Head `290c7a43...` 之上执行 WO-M1-2026-002 的 Must 项，不继承该 Head 的 RG0 结论。任何新 Head 上的 RG0/RG1 仍为 `PENDING-EXTERNAL-INDEPENDENT-REVIEW`。Draft 自动化校验结构、页账完整性、来源登记散列／角色绑定、封闭的 profileScope/rhoRA/activation 常量以及断言覆盖；它仍不核验合并拓扑，也不制造独立批准。`mergeEvidence` 在外部评审者批准不变 Head 之前保持 `NOT-YET-PRESENT`。

RFC 768／791／1123／1350／1785／2347／2348／2349 与 ARINC 664 第 2、3 部分现已作为开放依赖登记，DD-018 列入受控技术方向决策。登记 PDF 的每一页均由 section span 或明确排除区间入账。
