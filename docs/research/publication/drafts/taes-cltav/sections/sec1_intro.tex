\section{Introduction}
\label{sec:intro}

Airborne software loaders exchange INFORMATION and UPLOAD traffic under declared
timers, file identities and operator actions~\cite{arinc615a3}. Loading
frameworks also treat identity, integrity, interrupted loading and version
conflict as connected engineering concerns~\cite{marques2021}. Consider, as a
hypothetical scenario rather than a cited protocol theorem, a FIND whose
acknowledgement is missing or late: the observation may be incomplete rather
than a clean protocol FAIL, and the tester must still choose a later
protocol-legal action under a finite resource. The engineering problem is not
merely to collect more tests, but to decide whether the current evidence is
usable and which later action is worth its charge.

This paper studies Closed-Loop Test--Analysis Verification (CL-TAV). Three
reader questions are used throughout:
\begin{itemize}
\item CL-RQ1 (loop consistency): do prediction, interpretation and candidate
update stay mutually consistent under one uncertainty reference?
\item CL-RQ2 (comparative effect): under the same budget and denominators, does
the closed loop change detection or isolation relative to Test-only,
Analysis-only and fixed Test-then-Analysis arms?
\item CL-RQ3 (uncertainty and stopping): how do interval timing, \texttt{ERROR},
Prep/Recover and exclusive stops affect those comparisons?
\end{itemize}

The contributions map onto those questions.
\begin{enumerate}
\item C1 (CL-RQ1): mutual consistency of uncertainty-aware interpretation,
timing verdict and whole-history compatible update in the declared finite
domain. Its true-fault retention statement is conditional on the model,
ownership, pairing and conservative-observation hypotheses R1--R5, not a
transplanted general diagnosis theorem~\cite{koehl2023,fraenzle2026,sampath1998}.
\item C2 (CL-RQ2, CL-RQ3): a prior-free one-step worst-case candidate-set choice
among feasible affordable tests, with explicit Prep/Recover, \texttt{ERROR},
resource and stop rules, evaluated against Test-only, Analysis-only and fixed
Test-then-Analysis arms. C2 is not a global multi-step optimum and need not
shrink the candidate set on every outcome~\cite{chanthery2009,hu2020,miao2025}.
\end{enumerate}

Requirements-based generation, conformance testing and mutation adequacy supply
important test-construction and evaluation
tools~\cite{tretmans1996,petrenko2016,jia2011,yang2025}. Active diagnosis already
closes observation--control loops, while real-time diagnosis and testing already
address uncertain communication timing. The narrower question here is whether
the declared 615A action, observation, cost and stop contracts form a useful and
empirically defensible combination. Aviation loading work supplies context, not
executed 615A evidence~\cite{chanthery2019,efkemann2011,sartaj2021}.

The evaluation uses four arms (CL-T, CL-A, CL-TA, CL-LOOP) and three registered
families (DETECT, LOCATE, ABLATION). Confirmatory numbers are not available.
