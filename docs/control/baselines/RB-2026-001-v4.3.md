## Meaning of "candidate"

v4.3 is a GVS-bound ARINC 615A Profile/Binding migration candidate.
Until a reviewed gate approves it and the PR merges, `RB-2026-001-v4.2.1`
remains the effective and frozen legacy repository baseline. v4.3 has no active
semantic authority, does not retroactively alter evidence, and earns no method
compatibility, instance-evaluation, certification, or authority-acceptance claim.

## Core semantic change

This candidate:

1. binds the instance to an immutable external Candidate GVS Core definition;
2. separates Core, Conformance-Testing Profile, ARINC 615A Product Binding, and
   Project Configuration ownership;
3. reclassifies the pre-framework ARINC architecture as Profile/Binding/project
   candidate content;
4. establishes temporary object mapping and migration-impact records;
5. preserves non-automatic promotion boundaries between result, Evidence Item,
   Objective Satisfaction, claim, compliance status, and authority acceptance;
6. establishes instance-finding output and a later third-handshake route;
7. leaves v4.2/v4.2.1 mathematics and frozen history unchanged.

The four-layer contract is
[`ARINC615A_PROFILE_BINDING_CONFIGURATION.md`](../contracts/ARINC615A_PROFILE_BINDING_CONFIGURATION.md).
L0–L7, A0–A4, R0–R5, RG, and G remain ARINC/Profile/project candidate
taxonomies and are not Generic GVS Core architecture or authority levels.

## Pre-framework proposal provenance

The original PR #9 head `53a98447bcfa862f082ce443d69115067d3ff2f1`
proposed L0–L7, A/R, VO, OSR, CEI, and related objects before the external
framework binding existed. After method-repository PR #14 merged, correction
commits reclassified that proposal under the external GVS Core authority
boundary. They do not rewrite the eight historical commits or claim the
original proposal was framework-based. `RB-2026-001-v4.2.1` remains the active,
frozen legacy baseline; v4.3 remains non-authoritative until review and merge.

## Status semantics

Execution validity, case verdict, objective status, and compliance status are
distinct. A single `PASS` never automatically promotes a requirement or
compliance claim.

## Migration rules

1. `RB-2026-001-v4.2.1` remains frozen.
2. v4.3 does not retroactively alter previous evidence.
3. historical T0–T3 labels are not silently converted.
4. existing raw evidence may be referenced by v4.3 only with explicit
   re-evaluation under the new objective and closure contracts.
5. timing formulas and robust timing semantics remain unchanged.
6. no existing empirical claim is promoted by the restructuring alone.
7. no certification credit is gained by adopting the new architecture.
8. L0–L7, A0–A4, R0–R5, RG, and G remain local candidate semantics;
9. compatibility remains `NOT-DETERMINED` and evaluation `NOT-EXERCISED` until
   separate reviewed evidence changes those states.

## Required non-claims

- certification-oriented does not mean certification-approved;
- internal assurance gates are not authority review gates;
- protocol conformance does not establish aircraft-level airworthiness
  compliance;
- mutation adequacy is not a certification objective;
- Bayesian or statistical calibration is not required to establish ordinary
  protocol verification evidence;
- tool qualification credit is not claimed unless explicitly established under
  an applicable qualification basis;
- aircraft and system-level certification requirements are not fabricated in the
  protocol-only study.

## “候选”的含义

v4.3 是 GVS 绑定的 ARINC 615A Profile/Binding 迁移候选。在受评审门禁批准且 PR 合并前，
`RB-2026-001-v4.2.1` 仍为已生效并冻结的 legacy 仓库基线。v4.3 尚无生效的语义权威，
不回溯修改证据，也不取得方法兼容性、实例评价、认证或权威接受主张。

## 核心语义变化

本候选固定绑定外部 Candidate GVS Core；分离 Core、Conformance-Testing Profile、ARINC
615A Product Binding 与 Project Configuration；把 pre-framework ARINC 架构重新归类为
Profile/Binding/project 候选；建立临时对象映射和迁移影响；保持 result、Evidence Item、
Objective Satisfaction、claim、compliance 和 authority acceptance 之间的非自动晋级边界；
建立实例 finding 与后续第三次握手入口；不改变 v4.2/v4.2.1 的数学和冻结历史。

四层契约见 [`ARINC615A_PROFILE_BINDING_CONFIGURATION.md`](../contracts/ARINC615A_PROFILE_BINDING_CONFIGURATION.md)。
L0–L7、A0–A4、R0–R5、RG 和 G 都是 ARINC/Profile/项目候选 taxonomy，不是 Generic
GVS Core 架构或权威层级。

## 前框架提案来源

原 PR #9 head `53a98447bcfa862f082ce443d69115067d3ff2f1` 在外部框架绑定建立前提出了
L0–L7、A/R、VO、OSR、CEI 等对象。方法仓库 PR #14 合并后，correction commits 将其
重新归类并置于外部 GVS Core 权威边界下；它们不改写八笔历史提交，也不声称原提案当时已
基于该框架。`RB-2026-001-v4.2.1` 仍是活动且冻结的 legacy 基线；v4.3 在评审和合并前
仍无生效权威。
