\begin{abstract}
Airborne software loading over ARINC~615A-3 is judged from timed
acknowledgements, exclusive file-system locks, and a write-then-commit order.
A missed acknowledgement or a lock timeout can leave the tester with an
incomplete observation, after which an unconstrained follow-up action can
violate the protocol lifecycle. This paper specifies Closed-Loop
Test--Analysis Verification (CL-TAV): a charged session that keeps a
history-compatible candidate set, selects one admissible TEST, Prep, Recover
or Admit action by a one-step minimax rule, and stops under exclusive named
classes. Under conservative observation and exclusive ownership, a
history-compatible update never reintroduces a previously eliminated
candidate (C1). The same one-step rule is specified for a named cost (C2). A
protocol-bound evaluation design records DETECT, LOCATE and ABLATION analyses
with declared denominators. Confirmatory measurements have not yet been
obtained; this manuscript does not claim measured improvement or
certification.
\end{abstract}
