# Certification Evidence Basis

| Field | Value |
|---|---|
| **Version** | 1.0 |
| **Baseline** | RB-2026-001-v4.3 |
| **Purpose** | separate authority/development-assurance basis from research literature |

This document groups the evidence basis for the certification-oriented
architecture. Authority guidance is distinguished from academic papers, and
research-only literature is labeled accordingly. Nothing here asserts that the
project has obtained authority approval.

## Airworthiness and development-assurance guidance

- FAA AC 20-115D
- FAA Order 8110.49A
- EASA AMC 20-115D
- FAA AC 20-174
- SAE ARP4754A
- SAE ARP4754B / EUROCAE ED-79B
- EASA development-assurance certification memoranda and guidance as applicable
- CAAC AC-21-2
- CAAC AC-21-AA-2024-41

Purpose: objective, activity, evidence structure; authority review; sampling;
configuration; test article, setup, and procedure conformity; development
assurance; traceability.

## Software, tool, and formal-method assurance

- RTCA DO-178C / EUROCAE ED-12C
- RTCA DO-248C / EUROCAE ED-94C
- RTCA DO-330 / EUROCAE ED-215
- RTCA DO-331 / EUROCAE ED-218
- RTCA DO-333 / EUROCAE ED-216

Purpose: verification objectives; lifecycle evidence; tool qualification;
model and formal-method context.

## Aviation communication and interface assurance

- FAA AC 20-156

Purpose: databus and interface requirements; integration; configuration;
failure and recovery; verification evidence.

## Protocol conformance testing standards

- ISO/IEC 9646-1, -2, -4, -5, -6, -7
- ETSI TR 102 840

Purpose: conformance concepts; abstract test suites; test architecture;
implementation conformance statements; model-based testing in standards.

## Formal and model-based testing literature

- Tretmans and ioco literature
- Timmer, Brinksma, Stoelinga — Model-Based Testing
- Alur and Dill — A Theory of Timed Automata
- Springintveld, Vaandrager, and D'Argenio — Testing Timed Automata
- protocol FSM conformance-testing literature

## Measurement and conformity assessment

- JCGM 100
- JCGM 106

Purpose: measurement uncertainty; conformity decisions under uncertainty.

## Research-only adequacy and diagnosis

- mutation-testing literature
- finite fault-domain literature
- held-out fault evaluation
- statistical calibration
- diagnosis and classification references

These are research support, not certification requirements.

## Engineering evidence architecture

- NASA Systems Engineering Handbook

Engineering best practice only, not an aviation authority basis.

# 中文版

| 字段 | 内容 |
|---|---|
| **版本** | 1.0 |
| **基线** | RB-2026-001-v4.3 |
| **目的** | 将权威/开发保证基础与研究文献分离 |

本文档汇总面向认证架构的证据基础。权威指南与学术论文相区分，仅研究类文献据此标注。此处不含项目已获权威批准的任何表述。

## 适航与开发保证指南

FAA AC 20-115D、FAA Order 8110.49A、EASA AMC 20-115D、FAA AC 20-174、SAE ARP4754A、SAE ARP4754B/EUROCAE ED-79B、EASA 开发保证适航备忘与指南（视适用）、CAAC AC-21-2、CAAC AC-21-AA-2024-41。

用途：目标、活动、证据结构；权威评审；抽样；配置；测试件/装置/规程符合性；开发保证；追踪。

## 软件、工具与形式方法保证

RTCA DO-178C/EUROCAE ED-12C、DO-248C/ED-94C、DO-330/ED-215、DO-331/ED-218、DO-333/ED-216。

用途：验证目标；生命周期证据；工具鉴定；模型与形式方法背景。

## 航空通信与接口保证

FAA AC 20-156。用途：数据总线与接口需求；集成；配置；故障与恢复；验证证据。

## 协议符合性测试标准

ISO/IEC 9646-1、-2、-4、-5、-6、-7；ETSI TR 102 840。用途：符合性概念；抽象测试集；测试架构；实现符合性声明；标准中的模型测试。

## 形式与模型测试文献

Tretmans/ioco 文献；Timmer、Brinksma、Stoelinga 模型测试；Alur 与 Dill 时间自动机理论；Springintveld、Vaandrager、D'Argenio 时间自动机测试；协议 FSM 符合性测试文献。

## 测量与符合性评定

JCGM 100、JCGM 106。用途：测量不确定度；不确定度下的符合性决策。

## 仅研究的充分性与诊断

变异测试、有限故障域、留出故障评价、统计校准、诊断与分类文献。这些是研究支持，非认证要求。

## 工程证据架构

NASA 系统工程手册。仅为工程最佳实践，非航空权威基础。
