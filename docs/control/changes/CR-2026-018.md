## Controlled scientific boundary

- C1 is mutual consistency and conditional true-hypothesis retention in the
  declared finite model under ownership, pairing and conservative observation
  assumptions. It is not a new universal soundness/completeness theorem for
  drift, loss, duplication, spurious events or reordering.
- Köhl--Hermanns is treated as the strongest C1 neighbour: passive possible-run
  diagnosis under latency, clock mapping and out-of-order observation, with
  exact and bounded-history variants kept distinct. Its exact-once and A4
  premises are not inherited, and its active prefixes are not active tests.
- C2 is a prior-free one-step minimax choice over the current finite feasible
  and affordable test set, plus the existing Prep/Recover/ERROR/resource/stop
  lifecycle. It is neither a first active-diagnosis loop nor a globally optimal
  multi-step policy.
- F-LAS is a proposed requirements/procedure framework, not executed ARINC 615A
  conformance evidence. IMA workload ratios are estimates of remaining work,
  not observed savings. Cockpit-display precision is version-bound and shares
  OCL with the comparator; it is not recall, localization accuracy or
  independent truth. Equivalent and undecided mutants remain visible, and the
  JAXEN percentage is not a CL-TAV prior.
