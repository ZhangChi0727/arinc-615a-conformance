\section{Experimental Setup and Evaluation Protocol}
\label{sec:setup}

No confirmatory execution is reported. This section records the bound, the
arms and the scoring contract. Figure~\ref{fig:exp} is the experiment
architecture: E0--E6 are registered experiment steps, not execution evidence.
Truth meets algorithm output only at the evaluator filter.

The protocol bound is UPLOAD and INFORMATION under ARINC~615A-3, with FIND,
SEND and ABORT as UPLOAD operations. INFORMATION is the load-list / LCL
operation. ARINC~645 identity is bound only for triggered leaves. T5 uses a
closed timing interval: containment is PASS, disjointness FAIL, partial
overlap INCONCLUSIVE. FIND clocks (2\,s / 3\,s) are not waived by abort.

Four arms form a \(2\times 2\) of closed loop and adaptive choice
(Table~\ref{tab:arms}). CL-T is open-loop fixed order. CL-A is adaptive
choice without committing feedback to \(H\). CL-TA is the specified loop.
CL-LOOP is closed-loop execution with a declared non-adaptive choice. All
arms share the same scene construction, stop classes and T5 oracle.

DETECT compares miss and refusal under the same detection budget and the T5
oracle; FIND abort does not waive the 2\,s / 3\,s clocks. LOCATE compares
surviving \(|H|\) and true-fault exclusion on confirmed in-domain injections.
ABLATION uses the registered factors: no feedback (CL-T or CL-TA); Prep/Recover
uncharged; interval versus point-timestamp on the same traces with clocks
fixed; and FIND-abort-as-waiver as a forbidden negative control, not an allowed
algorithm. Sample size remains a named gap.

An experimental unit is a unique registered attempt on one base scene, arm and
control/fault construction. Development, pilot and held-out lineages are
disjoint; paired arms require independent reset evidence. Equivalence work is
not scored as killed~\cite{gruen2009,jia2011}. When later measurements exist,
interval and effect reporting follows the registered unit~\cite{arcuri2011}.
Clock and error budgets, when frozen, follow the declared metrology
context~\cite{jcgm100,jcgm106}.

\paragraph{Accounting contract.}
Every later cell names a finite population, a missing/censoring rule and the
empty-denominator behaviour. With registered attempts and no answers, answered
coverage is \(0/N\) and answered-conditional error is N/A. Abstain is not
PASS/FAIL. A record without a run identifier is a pre-run exclusion and cannot
produce PASS, FAIL, a decision, an action, or charged cost. An update rejected
as ERROR preserves \(H\) regardless of the stop tag; only a valid empty
(resp.\ singleton) update yields Stop-Empty (resp.\ Stop-Singleton).
Evaluator truth is not a session input.

For the registered no-response FIND scenario, the scored trigger is the FIND
timeout under T5, not a later abort. A prescribed abort after that timeout is
not scored against the no-response obligation; the abort is a fallback, not a
waiver of the 2\,s / 3\,s clocks. The supplement lists the corresponding
repository identifiers.
