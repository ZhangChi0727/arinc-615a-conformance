## Scope

This path teaches reusable verification reasoning without assuming ARINC 615A
or the repository's Python implementation. It distinguishes Verification from
Validation; Test and Analysis from Review and Inspection; requirements,
models, cases, observations, evidence, inference, and claims; and deterministic
timing obligations from statistical repeated-run questions.

## Planned modules

1. verification scope, non-claims, and evidence questions;
2. requirements, verification-method allocation, and traceability;
3. behavioral and timed models;
4. Test design, oracles, and four-valued verdicts;
5. Analysis of coverage, finite fault domains, uncertainty, and validity;
6. Review and Inspection gates;
7. reproducibility, evidence manifests, and bounded claim writing.

The local source notes are research inputs only. Modules are rewritten against
controlled terminology and independently reviewed before publication.

## Traceability and reuse

Each lesson records a tutorial ID/version, concept sources, the methodology
baseline used for project-specific wording, learning objectives, exercises, and
known boundaries. A lesson may be reused by the ARINC 615A tutorial through its
stable lesson ID; duplicated prose is avoided.

## 范围

本路径教授可复用的验证推理，不假设 ARINC 615A 或仓库 Python 实现。它区分 Verification 与 Validation、Test/Analysis 与 Review/Inspection、需求/模型/用例/观测/证据/推断/主张，以及确定性时序义务与统计性重复运行问题。

## 计划模块

1. 验证范围、非主张和证据问题；
2. 需求、验证方法分配和追踪；
3. 行为与时序模型；
4. 测试设计、oracle 和四值判定；
5. 覆盖、有限故障域、不确定性和有效性分析；
6. 评审与检查门；
7. 可复现性、证据清单和有边界的主张写作。

本地参考资料仅作为研究输入。模块必须按照受控术语重写，并在发布前接受独立评审。
