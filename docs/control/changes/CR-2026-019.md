## Objective and authority split

This change refines the accepted C1/C2 claims, four arms, three experiment
families and eight scenarios into a reviewable implementation design. The
active method, algorithm/interface registry, development contracts and
`EXP-PLAN-2026-001` remain their respective authorities. The bilingual
implementation specification is the human design attachment. The closed JSON
is a finite cross-reference and accounting index; its schema or checker cannot
approve novelty, proof, fairness or execution.

## Bound inputs

The design index records an exact role, repository-relative path, Git blob OID
and SHA-256 for nine inputs: interface registry, development contracts, claim
matrix, experiment plan, method report, manuscript manifest, M1, M2 and the
literature evidence ledger. Accepted authorities are distinguished from
successor baselines revised by this change. Hashes are over raw Git-blob bytes
at the base, not a dirty working-tree rendering; authority consumers parse the
verified bytes. The bindings identify inputs and do not self-reference the
eventual PR head.

## Outputs and controlled boundary

Outputs are this CR, DD-043, the claim/plan refinements, the bilingual
implementation specification, the closed JSON/schema, a bounded checker and
tests, and manuscript placeholder alignment. C1 remains conditional
interpretation/history consistency and true-hypothesis retention under R1--R5;
C2 remains the existing one-step, prior-free minimax ranking over eligible and
affordable TEST actions. Specification conformance and empirical benefit are
separate propositions.

The design preserves CL-T, CL-A, CL-TA and CL-LOOP; EXP-CLTAV-DETECT,
EXP-CLTAV-LOCATE and EXP-CLTAV-ABLATION; the eight existing scenarios; and all
14 acceptance-matrix categories. It does not activate FIND, DOWNLOAD or AFDX,
does not add a fifth arm, and does not alter R1--R5, A1--A5, P1--P5, S0--S10,
algorithms, records, interfaces, CRS, M2 or engineering contracts.

## Admission and dependencies

Design review is distinct from run admission. Confirmatory execution remains
blocked by named decisions for independent mathematical review, an interactive
IUT and reset contract, truth/injection confirmation, equivalent-fault
adjudication, a justified sample/repeat decision, a resource/stop budget and a
clock/error budget. Historical captures
remain exploratory offline evidence; synthetic fixtures are accounting tests.
The dependency matrix identifies existing fields and proposed contract deltas
for later Decision Maker routing. It does not change or task PR #22.
The latest correction makes the safe scene projection a typed member allowlist,
adds explicit truth-scorable and missing-truth flows, derives pair eligibility
per parent metric from construction and confirmed-reset evidence, makes update
status control ERROR preservation and empty/singleton priority, binds the
CRS-M1-00101 status-file/abort timeline, and derives statistical branches from
independence, pairing or clustering inputs with exact source ranges. Pre-run
review/setup effort remains visible but is not arm cost.

## Acceptance and non-authorizations

Acceptance requires the work order AC-01--AC-20, protected-object zero diff,
local and final-head CI gates, readable main/supplement PDFs and independent
review. This CR does not authorize a registered or executable experiment,
confirmatory runs, scientific or mathematical approval, Configuration,
capability, conformance, certification, Ready status, merge, tag or submission.

# CR-2026-019——CL-TAV 实验实施设计
