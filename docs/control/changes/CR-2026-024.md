## Objective and controlled scope

Authorize a process-local, deterministic bound-model simulated 615A target for
UPLOAD FIND/SEND/ABORT and INFORMATION, plus a declared single-fault `H_single`
injector, independent evaluator truth store, injection confirmation, per-attempt
reset evidence, and a simulated clock with declared ε. Every artefact is labelled
SIMULATED-IUT. No manuscript result cell is filled. Phase B arms and Phase C/D
pipeline/pilot are out of scope.

## Authorized deliverables

- Bound-model simulated IUT in `src/a615a_sim/` with no DOWNLOAD/AFDX behaviour.
- Fault catalogue table and injector for declared `H_single` plus named evaluator
  constructions that are not in `H_single`.
- IF-EXP-INJECT confirmation (planned ≠ confirmed) and IF-EXP-TRUTH records
  written only after confirmation, with a fail-closed leakage guard.
- DEP-RESET evidence fields (`pairingId`, `resetId`, `resetEvidenceRef`) on the
  simulated target.
- Simulated DEP-CLOCK with declared ε and EXP-PLAN T5 interval treatment.

## Boundaries and non-authorizations

- Does not change `src/cltav_runtime/` semantics, CRS, M2, algorithm interfaces,
  four-arm adapters, or confirmatory registration.
- Does not close DEC-IUT, DEC-TRUTH, DEC-CLOCK, DEC-EQUIV, DEC-SAMPLE, or
  DEC-RESOURCE. Simulated instruments are not physical calibration or real IUT
  evidence.
- Does not fill PH-RES-* / PH-ABS-RESULT / PH-STAT-N or claim conformance,
  certification, real-IUT behaviour, or method superiority.
- Does not start Phase B/C/D, Ready, merge, tags, or protection changes.

## Fault catalogue (SIMULATED-IUT)

| ID | In `H_single` | Scene | Scope | Mechanism | Observable signature |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| H-NORMAL | no | SC-NORMAL-UPLOAD | UPLOAD | no perturbation | FIND then COMPLETED |
| H-WAIT-CONTINUE | yes | SC-WAIT-CONTINUE | UPLOAD | WAIT then delayed CONTINUE | WAIT and CONTINUE with obligation retained |
| H-NO-RESPONSE | yes | SC-NO-RESPONSE | INFORMATION | suppress next status and abort fallback after delay | STATUS-WITH-DELAY then NO-RESPONSE |
| H-ABORT | yes | SC-ABORT | UPLOAD | owned ABORT during SEND | ABORT with active SEND correlation |
| H-SAME-KEY | yes | SC-SAME-KEY | INFORMATION | reuse live correlation key | two INFORMATION requests share one key |
| H-SINGLE-BATCH | yes | SC-SINGLE-BATCH | UPLOAD | batch policy on otherwise identical reset | publicConfigurationRef BATCH |
| H-INVALID-OBS | no | SC-INVALID-OBS | INFORMATION | evaluator corrupts timestamp/ownership | missing correlation id |
| H-RESOURCE-ERROR | no | SC-RESOURCE-ERROR | INFORMATION | named tool/resource error | RESOURCE-ERROR with spent cost retained |

`H_single` is taken as the in-domain single protocol/configuration faults among
the eight registered scenes. SC-INVALID-OBS and SC-RESOURCE-ERROR remain
evaluator/tool constructions, not IUT hypotheses. DEC-EQUIV stays OPEN, so
equivalenceDisposition is `unresolved`.

## 目标与受控范围

授权一个进程内、确定性的有界模型模拟 615A 目标（UPLOAD 的 FIND／SEND／ABORT 与
INFORMATION），以及声明的单故障 `H_single` 注入器、独立评价者真值存储、注入确认、
每次尝试的复位证据，和带声明 ε 的模拟时钟。全部制品标注 SIMULATED-IUT。不填写稿件
结果格。Phase B 对照臂与 Phase C／D 流水线／试点不在范围。

## 授权交付

- `src/a615a_sim/` 中的有界模拟 IUT，不含 DOWNLOAD／AFDX。
- 故障目录表与注入器；评价者构造不进入 `H_single`。
- IF-EXP-INJECT 确认（计划 ≠ 确认）以及仅在确认后写入的 IF-EXP-TRUTH；泄漏守卫失败即关闭。

- 带声明 ε 的模拟 DEP-CLOCK 与实验方案 T5 区间处理。

## 边界与未授权

- 不改变 `src/cltav_runtime/` 语义、CRS、M2、算法接口、四臂适配器或确认性登记。
- 不关闭 DEC-IUT、DEC-TRUTH、DEC-CLOCK、DEC-EQUIV、DEC-SAMPLE 或 DEC-RESOURCE。
- 不填写结果占位，不主张符合性、认证、真实 IUT 行为或方法优越性。

## 故障目录（SIMULATED-IUT）

| ID | 属于 `H_single` | 场景 | 范围 | 机制 | 可观察签名 |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| H-NORMAL | 否 | SC-NORMAL-UPLOAD | UPLOAD | 无扰动 | FIND 后 COMPLETED |
| H-WAIT-CONTINUE | 是 | SC-WAIT-CONTINUE | UPLOAD | WAIT 后延迟 CONTINUE | WAIT 与 CONTINUE，义务保留 |
| H-NO-RESPONSE | 是 | SC-NO-RESPONSE | INFORMATION | 延迟后抑制下一状态和中止后备 | STATUS-WITH-DELAY 然后 NO-RESPONSE |
| H-ABORT | 是 | SC-ABORT | UPLOAD | SEND 期间有主 ABORT | 与活动 SEND 同键的 ABORT |
| H-SAME-KEY | 是 | SC-SAME-KEY | INFORMATION | 复用活动相关键 | 两次 INFORMATION 共用一键 |
| H-SINGLE-BATCH | 是 | SC-SINGLE-BATCH | UPLOAD | 在其余相同复位上选用批量策略 | publicConfigurationRef 为 BATCH |
| H-INVALID-OBS | 否 | SC-INVALID-OBS | INFORMATION | 评价者破坏时间戳／归属 | 缺相关标识 |
| H-RESOURCE-ERROR | 否 | SC-RESOURCE-ERROR | INFORMATION | 具名工具／资源错误 | RESOURCE-ERROR 并保留已花费成本 |

`H_single` 取八个已登记场景中的域内单协议／配置故障。SC-INVALID-OBS 与
SC-RESOURCE-ERROR 仍是评价者／工具构造，不是 IUT 假设。DEC-EQUIV 保持 OPEN，因此
equivalenceDisposition 为 `unresolved`。

Truth-store 直接调用方仅限 `a615a_sim.evaluator` / `a615a_sim.faults`；测试前缀不再放行。
读写须持有由 `issue_truth_capability()` 签发的 `TruthCapability`；无令牌或伪造令牌失败关闭。
测试经评价者入口完成读写，不把真值当作算法输入。
