\section{Results}
\label{sec:results}

No effect direction, percentage or superiority is reported.

Every future cell is derived from unique \texttt{attemptId} records. Its
completion record must identify the claim/RQ, target subset, numerator,
denominator, filtering, metric-specific zero-denominator rule, missingness,
budget censoring, source
fields, unit and charged cost projection. Overlapping flow labels are not
summed as an exclusive partition. The synthetic design ledger validates these
rules only and is excluded from all result panels.

Coverage metrics retain the finite all-attempt denominator: zero answered
attempts among \(N>0\) are reported as \(0/N\). Metrics conditional on the
answered subset, such as conditional detection error, are N/A when that subset
is empty. Every localization panel first reports decision coverage; truth
retention/exclusion and singleton correctness do not silently discard eligible
attempts that ended without a decision.
