Köhl and Hermanns are the strongest C1 neighbour found. Their passive diagnoser
ranges over runs compatible with the whole timed observation history under
bounded action-specific latency and a clock remapping, including latency-induced
reordering~\cite{koehl2023}. Their exact incremental algorithm is separated from
a sound but generally incomplete fixed-history approximation. CL-TAV therefore
does not claim history compatibility or uncertainty handling as new, does not
call their ``active prefixes'' active tests, and does not inherit exact-once
observation or their liveness assumption. Fränzle~\textit{et al.} likewise give
exact delay/jitter-aware monitoring and testing semantics, but testing there is
harness input plus verdict evaluation rather than adaptive next-action
optimization~\cite{fraenzle2026}.

Active diagnosis has long combined online state estimation with controlled
behaviour~\cite{sampath1998}. Conditional action plans, state-conditioned event
disablement and time-interval restrictions further establish feedback and
active choice as prior art~\cite{chanthery2009,hu2020,miao2025}. Some objectives
use costs or probabilistic information. CL-TAV instead fixes the current finite
feasible set and ranks an affordable distinguishing TEST by the one-step worst
residual cardinality, then cost and identifier. The lack of a default prior is a
design choice, not an automatic advantage; Prep/Recover and exclusive error/stop
semantics are compared as engineering contract differences, not proof of first
combination or global optimality.

For the application bridge, onboard active diagnosis demonstrates procedure
generation and also reports wrong localization under limited observability and
model/prototype mismatch~\cite{chanthery2019}. IMA and cockpit-display work
demonstrate configuration-aware, model-derived avionics testing
\cite{efkemann2011,sartaj2021}. F-LAS proposes 23 reusable requirements and six
procedures across normal, interrupted/incomplete and version-conflict scenarios,
but reports neither procedure execution nor an ARINC 615A conformance result
\cite{marques2021}. These sources motivate cases and threats; the standard and
the controlled CRS remain the protocol authority. All comparisons above are
bound to the full-text versions recorded in the evidence ledger; no absence
claim is inferred from an unreported feature.

\subsection{Verification objects}
The IUT is a 615A data-loading endpoint. The bound protocol model is UPLOAD
and INFORMATION~\cite{arinc615a3}. FIND, DOWNLOAD and conditional AFDX appear
in the CRS; they are not implemented behaviour and not an experimental arm.

Candidates are \(H_0=\{h_{\mathrm{normal}}\}\cup H_{\mathrm{single}}\).
An observable summary \(q\) is known or unknown. Observation classes are
projected onto current \(H\). Measurement uncertainty is a declared
conservative outer set \(U\) in the GUM sense~\cite{jcgm100,jcgm106}, not an
estimated physical error model. Resource is exactly one mode:
BUDGET\((c_{\min},B)\) or ROUNDS\((K_{\max})\).

A 615A-triggered 645 bind covers CRC, check-value and naming
leaves~\cite{arinc6451}. Integrity capabilities remain unestablished.
Stop-645 names a remaining 645-dependent obligation, not a missing file.
