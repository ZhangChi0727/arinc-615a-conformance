# CL-TAV Manuscript Audit

Use this skill for manuscript review, evidence-grounded revision, related-work
integration, and pre-submission checks. Do not use it to create experimental
results, approve mathematical claims, or declare submission readiness.

Read the publication guide, manuscript manifest, claim-evidence matrix, and the
sections in scope. For material factual or comparative statements, inspect the
claim-citation and evidence ledgers. Distinguish `observed`, `reported`,
`derived`, `inferred`, `planned`, and `unverified` statements.

Preserve the project's negative boundaries: no invented confirmatory values,
no unearned protocol conformance or certification claim, no silent promotion of
open-source implementations, and no claim that unsuccessful searching proves
novelty. Keep placeholders explicit until their completion conditions are met.

Audit content before polishing prose. Language edits must preserve qualifiers,
units, variables, algorithm duties, citation scope, and source identity. Apply
IEEE TAES conventions and the manuscript's own vocabulary rather than imitating
Nature house style.

Read [references/audit-contract.md](references/audit-contract.md) for the audit
passes and result categories.
