\section{Admit and post-update stops}
A1 selects one affordable, eligible, distinguishing action from the exclusive
TEST, Prep or Recover alternatives. A2 is KNOWN with a
distinguishing TEST or eligible Prep, but no such action affordable
(Stop-Budget). A3 is KNOWN with no distinguishing TEST and no eligible Prep
(Stop-NoDistinguisher). A4 is UNKNOWN with no eligible Recover (Stop-Error).
A5 is UNKNOWN with Recover eligible but unaffordable (Stop-Budget). A TEST
prediction GAP is handled independently: a KNOWN GAP without eligible Prep is
SPEC-ERROR, not A3; retry-cap remains an independent Stop-Error condition.
P1 Stop-Empty; P2 Stop-Singleton; P3 Stop-645; P4 Stop-Equivalent;
P5 Stop-Budget after update. Stop-NoDistinguisher is exclusive with those
P-stops when no remaining distinguisher exists and no P-stop already fired.
Order is Empty, Singleton, 645, Equivalent, Budget. Singleton
\(h_{\mathrm{normal}}\) is not protocol PASS.

\section{Short arguments}
Monotonicity, retention (R1)--(R5), one-step minimax and resource termination
are stated in the main text. The algebraic details are the accepted method
§3.9.1 arguments: update is intersection; \texttt{ERROR} does not exclude;
minimax is a least element of a finite selectable set; billed executions are
bounded by the selected mode. They are not global optimality and not
independent mathematical approval.
