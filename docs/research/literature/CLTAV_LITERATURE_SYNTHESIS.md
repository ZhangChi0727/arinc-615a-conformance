## Nearest work and residual risk

### C1: uncertain timing, partial observation, and compatible-history update

1. **Köhl & Hermanns (2023)** is the strongest direct C1 neighbour. It defines possible true runs under bounded action-specific latency, clock drift/offset, and out-of-order arrivals; supplies an exact incremental algorithm that is sound and Delta-complete in the conditional Eq. (10) sense; and distinguishes that guarantee from a sound but generally incomplete bounded-history approximation with model-size-bounded insertion resources.
2. **Fränzle et al. (2026)** is the strongest verified neighbour for exact monitoring/testing under unknown bounded constant communication latency plus varying bounded jitter. It does not model clock drift and does not adaptively select the next diagnostic action.
3. **Miao, Komenda & Lai (2025)** is the strongest verified neighbour for partially observed time-interval automata with history-dependent state estimation and active event/time restriction.
4. **Tripakis (2002)** is the foundational verified neighbour for timed-automata diagnosis and compatible timed-state estimation under exact timed observations.

These uncertainty models must not be collapsed. Miao's intervals primarily constrain **system transition/event timing**. Tripakis assumes exact timed observations within its model. Fränzle and Köhl/Hermanns concern **observation/communication timing uncertainty**; Köhl/Hermanns additionally names drift and reordering. CL-TAV must state which of these it models and which guarantees it provides.

#### Source-internal timing consistency note

### D4: minimum fair-evaluation contract

The literature increment sharpens the experiment plan without authorizing execution or changing the registered method:

1. **Keep all four registered arms under one resource contract.** Each arm must receive the same candidate universe, initial information, legal action pool, wall-clock/accounting boundary, and declared budget. If a baseline cannot expose equivalent actions, outputs, or stopping rules, label it a contextual comparison rather than a performance arm.
2. **Keep the traffic ledger, detection answers, and localization results distinct.** The existing experiment contract already records six accounting labels—attempt, answered-subset, abstain, invalid-observation, equivalent-fault, and unconfirmed-injection. They serve different metrics and audit questions; they are not renamed into three mutually exclusive and exhaustive denominators. Any eligibility-conditioned result must name the subset and its exclusion rule rather than silently removing a registered attempt. `EVID-2026-061`–`062` supply only the cross-domain idea that coverage and conditional risk should be co-reported; they do not supply CL-TAV labels, truth, localization-success semantics, or an ML guarantee.

   | Reporting layer | Required interpretation |
   |---|---|
   | Overall traffic | Every registered attempt remains traceable. Unconfirmed injections and confirmed equivalent faults stay in the ledger even when they do not enter a detection or mutation-score denominator. If an eligibility-conditioned rate is also reported, name that subset and every exclusion. |
   | Detection answer | The detection answered subset contains valid PASS/FAIL answers. `INCONCLUSIVE`, `ERROR`, and abstention do not become PASS or FAIL. Detection error and miss rates use a declared valid-truth detection denominator. |
   | Localization | Report separately whether a localization decision was issued, whether it was correct, and the surviving-candidate-set size on a declared localization subset. A valid FAIL with multiple surviving candidates is a detection answer but not successful singleton localization; a singleton normal hypothesis is not protocol PASS. |
   | Unresolved and excluded flow | Preserve abstain, invalid-observation, equivalent-fault, unconfirmed-injection, and any undecided-equivalence counts with reasons. These are accounting sets for different purposes, not forced binary outcomes. |
   | Literature transfer | Selective prediction motivates co-reporting coverage and conditional error. It does not define CL-TAV ground truth, PASS/FAIL, candidate-set correctness, or localization success. |

   Illustrative accounting only—not experiment data: if 10 attempts are registered, 2 injections are unconfirmed, 6 of the remaining 8 yield valid PASS/FAIL, and 2 abstain, then both 6/10 all-attempt answer coverage and 6/8 answer coverage conditional on the explicitly named confirmed-injection subset may be shown; they are not the same unconditional metric. Likewise, a valid FAIL with residual set `{h1,h2}` is answered for detection but not singleton-localized, and a residual singleton containing only the normal hypothesis does not itself establish protocol PASS.
3. **Separate truth and discrepancy classes before scoring.** Confirm injected truth independently where possible and keep equivalent fault, unconfirmed injection, model/oracle mismatch, simulator limitation, invalid observation, extraction false positive, and product defect distinct. The 2019 CDS paper's simulator-functionality discrepancies (`EVID-2026-059`) and the 2021 extension's shared-OCL simulator comparison (`EVID-2026-073`–`075`) show two different ways that a convincing count can still answer a narrower question than end-to-end localization correctness.
4. **Register equivalent-fault handling before seeing outcomes.** Mutation score excludes confirmed equivalent mutants, but the experiment ledger must retain them, state who or what established equivalence, and preserve `UNDECIDED` or `UNCONFIRMED` rather than forcing a binary label (`EVID-2026-076`, `078`). The historical 10–40% survey range and the JAXEN 40% sample are not CL-TAV priors, quotas, or sample-size inputs.
5. **Use the experimental unit, not packets or screenshots, for uncertainty.** One independently generated fault/session/seed is the presumptive unit; repeated packets, timestamps, candidates, constraint failures, images, or observations inside it are dependent measurements. Sartaj et al. explicitly separate tens of thousands of images and failed constraints from unique constraint-level faults and seven grouped fault types (`EVID-2026-072`). The final unit still must be fixed in the preregistration.
6. **Condition statistical machinery on actual randomness.** If selection, injection, workload generation, or ordering is randomized, use independent runs and report success proportions separately from successful-run cost/time, including censoring at budget limits and effect sizes with uncertainty. If all registered cells are deterministic, Arcuri & Briand does not by itself require repeated random seeds. Its large-run examples are not a blanket sample-size prescription for expensive HIL tests (`EVID-2026-063`–`065`).
7. **Account for setup and recovery.** Model/oracle construction, configuration, simulator setup, instrumentation, equivalence review, Prep, Recover, invalid-run diagnosis, and reset must be either inside the measured cost boundary or separately reported. Do not compare a fully provisioned CL-TAV path against a baseline whose setup burden is omitted. Impact-based mutant prioritization may reduce review burden, but it changes the fault population and must be reported as a selection rule (`EVID-2026-079`–`080`).

These rules support future evaluation of `CL-LOCATE` and `CL-ABLATION`; they do not support either claim today. No external paper reviewed in Batch B is automatically a fair executable baseline.

## Suggested related-work narrative for a later manuscript round

Prior work has already established closed-loop active diagnosis, observation-history/state-estimate feedback, timed diagnosis, time-interval active control, cost-aware sequential test selection, and runtime monitoring/testing under communication-time uncertainty. Aerospace work further demonstrates executable onboard diagnostic procedures and shows that observability and model mismatch can produce wrong localization. Accordingly, CL-TAV should not present any of these ingredients as a category-level first.

CL-TAV still needs to demonstrate a narrower engineering and empirical proposition: under a declared ARINC 615A observation and timing model, its protocol-legal actions, whole-history residual update, explicit preparation/recovery/error and stop semantics, and prior-free budgeted selection yield trustworthy detection or localization evidence under fair comparisons. That value remains to be established with controlled executions, adverse cases, abstention-aware denominators, and reproducible timing evidence; it has not been achieved merely by specifying the method or completing this literature batch.

For the engineering bridge, F-LAS supplies a directly relevant proposed structure for loading identity, integrity, incomplete loading, version conflict, manual or automatic checks, and requirements-to-test traceability. Adjacent avionics work supports an explicit account of configuration, test level, simulator or hardware boundary, oracle construction, and preparation cost. None permits ARINC 661/653/666/667 results to be described as ARINC 615A validation. The final evaluation should publish all-attempt accounting plus separately named detection-answer and localization subsets, preserve invalid, equivalent, undecided, and abstained attempts in the accounting, and distinguish independent injected truth from model, simulator, extraction, and oracle discrepancies.

These three paragraphs are drafting advice only and are not inserted into the manuscript.
