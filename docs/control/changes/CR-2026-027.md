## Objective and controlled scope

Record the user ruling that the IUT for this CL-TAV study is the repository's
local simulated 615A environment (fault-injectable bound-model IUT, TH /
FIND-HOST side). No real-target experiment is planned. Close DEC-IUT only.
Keep DEC-TRUTH, DEC-CLOCK, DEC-EQUIV, DEC-SAMPLE and DEC-RESOURCE OPEN.
Label every subsequent experimental product SIMULATED-IUT. Do not claim
real-target behaviour, conformance or certification. This CR does not declare
pilot N or seeds and does not run the pilot.

## Authorized deliverables

- DD-051 recording DEC-IUT = local simulated environment.
- Status surfaces that DEC-IUT is closed under this CR; other DEC-* remain OPEN.

## Boundaries and non-authorizations

  interfaces.
- Does not close DEC-TRUTH, DEC-CLOCK, DEC-EQUIV, DEC-SAMPLE, or DEC-RESOURCE.
- Does not fill PH-RES-* / PH-ABS-RESULT / PH-STAT-N or claim superiority.
- Does not freeze confirmatory `registration.yaml`.
- Does not start Ready, merge, tags, or protection changes.
- Does not execute the Phase D census in this CR.
