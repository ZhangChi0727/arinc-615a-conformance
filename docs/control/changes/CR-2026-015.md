## Why this change exists

The method, algorithm contracts and experiment protocol are specified. The next
substantive delivery is a TAES Regular Paper first draft that a human reviewer
can compile and inspect, with reserved layout for unrun confirmatory results.

## Scope that is in

- A TAES Regular Paper English main manuscript and a separate supplementary PDF.
- Journalized eight-section structure aligned with C1/C2 and CL-RQ1–CL-RQ3.
- Project page budget: main PDF at most 10 pages including references, with
  reserved result-figure space; supplementary compiled separately.
- Explicit placeholders with unique IDs, dependencies and completion conditions.
- Author/affiliation/funding checklist with pending institutional inputs.
- Mechanical manuscript gates, owned-PDF registration and reader-entry updates.

## Scope that is out

- Codec, protocol engine, executable verification engine, core estimators or
  solvers, network I/O, HIL, AFDX hardware, confirmatory experiments, tags,
  Project Configuration, M3.
- Any change to CRS requirement content, M2 transitions or observational timing,
  bound source semantics, or accepted algorithm control logic.
- Submission to TAES, payment of over-length or OA fees, institutional ranking
  confirmation, fabricated results, or reprint of proprietary standard figures.
