## Objective and controlled scope

Authorize a process-local, deterministic bound-model simulated 615A target for
UPLOAD FIND/SEND/ABORT and INFORMATION, plus a declared single-fault `H_single`
injector, independent evaluator truth store, injection confirmation, per-attempt
reset evidence, and a simulated clock with declared ε. Every artefact is labelled
SIMULATED-IUT. No manuscript result cell is filled. Phase B arms and Phase C/D
pipeline/pilot are out of scope.

## Authorized deliverables

- Bound-model simulated IUT in `src/a615a_sim/` with no DOWNLOAD/AFDX behaviour.
- Fault catalogue table and injector for declared `H_single` plus named evaluator
  constructions that are not in `H_single`.
- IF-EXP-INJECT confirmation (planned ≠ confirmed) and IF-EXP-TRUTH records
  written only after confirmation, with a fail-closed leakage guard.
- DEP-RESET evidence fields (`pairingId`, `resetId`, `resetEvidenceRef`) on the
  simulated target.
- Simulated DEP-CLOCK with declared ε and EXP-PLAN T5 interval treatment.

## Boundaries and non-authorizations

- Does not change `src/cltav_runtime/` semantics, CRS, M2, algorithm interfaces,
  four-arm adapters, or confirmatory registration.
- Does not close DEC-IUT, DEC-TRUTH, DEC-CLOCK, DEC-EQUIV, DEC-SAMPLE, or
  DEC-RESOURCE. Simulated instruments are not physical calibration or real IUT
  evidence.
- Does not fill PH-RES-* / PH-ABS-RESULT / PH-STAT-N or claim conformance,
  certification, real-IUT behaviour, or method superiority.
- Does not start Phase B/C/D, Ready, merge, tags, or protection changes.

## Fault catalogue (SIMULATED-IUT)

IUT is the target side (TH / FIND-HOST). Data-loader behaviour is tester
infrastructure and is out of `H_single`. `H_single` is five scene-independent
target faults. WAIT/CONTINUE, owned ABORT, and BATCH are `h_normal` trajectories.
Confirmation tokens are per fault, not per scene. The injector overlays a fault
on any applicable scene and rejects inapplicable `(sceneId, faultId)` pairs.
The evaluator-only fault plan is the list of `(sceneId, faultId|h_normal)`.

| ID | In `H_single` | Basis | Applicable scenes | Mechanism | Observable signature | Expected T5/halt | Token |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| H-NORMAL | no | — | all eight registered scenes | no IUT overlay | scene trajectory without an `H_single` overlay | FIND host answer in CRS-M1-00391 `[0,2]` when FIND occurs | H-NORMAL |
| F-FIND-ANSWER-LATE | yes | CRS-M1-00391 | NORMAL, WAIT-CONTINUE, ABORT, SINGLE-BATCH | TH FIND-HOST answer after 2 s and before declared observation end | FIND-ANSWER-LATE tick in `(2, observationEnd)` | T5 FAIL vs `[0,2]`; continue to observation end | FIND-ANSWER-LATE |
| F-FIND-ANSWER-ABSENT | yes | CRS-M1-00391 | same as LATE | no TH FIND-HOST answer through observation end | FIND-ANSWER-ABSENT; no host-answer event | T5 FAIL vs `[0,2]`; halt at observation end | FIND-ANSWER-ABSENT |
| F-LUR-ACK-MISSING | yes | CRS-M1-00366 | same as LATE | TH omits ACK after matching LUR WRQ | LUR-WRQ present; no TH LUR-ACK | halt without TH LUR-ACK | LUR-ACK-MISSING |
| F-LUR-ACK-WITHOUT-WRQ | yes | CRS-M1-00366; TH-MATCHING-LUR-WRQ-RECEIVED | same as LATE | TH ACK while matching WRQ was not received | LUR-ACK-WITHOUT-WRQ; matchingWrqReceived=false | halt after unmatched ACK | LUR-ACK-WITHOUT-WRQ |
| F-STATUS-SILENCE | yes | CRS-M1-00106 | NO-RESPONSE, WAIT-CONTINUE, ABORT | TH omits next timed status; DL abort fallback not suppressed | STATUS-WITH-DELAY then STATUS-SILENCE; abortFallback=AVAILABLE | TH status unmet; DL abort fallback remains | STATUS-SILENCE |
| H-INVALID-OBS | no | — | SC-INVALID-OBS | evaluator corrupts timestamp/ownership | missing correlation id | not IUT-scored | MISSING-CORRELATION |
| H-RESOURCE-ERROR | no | — | SC-RESOURCE-ERROR | named tool/resource error | RESOURCE-ERROR with spent cost retained | not IUT-scored | RESOURCE-ERROR |
| H-SAME-KEY | no | — | SC-SAME-KEY | reuse live correlation key | two INFORMATION requests share one key | DETECT/ABLATION only | REUSED-KEY |

Declared observation end is `2 s + ε + margin + extra` (`DECLARED_OBSERVATION_END_S`)
so LATE and ABSENT have distinct signatures. `(F-FIND-ANSWER-LATE, F-FIND-ANSWER-ABSENT)`
is registered as a DEC-EQUIV candidate; DEC-EQUIV stays OPEN.

### Pilot-only LOCATE deviation (do not edit `cltav_experiment_design.json`)

SC-SAME-KEY, SC-RESOURCE-ERROR, and SC-INVALID-OBS are used for DETECT/ABLATION
accounting only in this pre-experiment; they are not used for LOCATE. Under the
controlled CRS they admit fewer than two in-domain hypotheses (SC-INVALID-OBS is
an evaluator construction). SC-NO-RESPONSE retains two hypotheses (`h_normal` and
F-STATUS-SILENCE); absence paths remain NOT-SCORED as designed. The FIND
registration `[0,3]` window is data-loader-side and is not published as a T5
verdict interval; the 2 s host answer remains `[0,2]` (CRS-M1-00391).

## 故障目录（SIMULATED-IUT）

IUT 为目标端（TH／FIND-HOST）。数据加载端为测试设施，不进入 `H_single`。
`H_single` 为五条与场景无关的目标故障。WAIT／CONTINUE、自有 ABORT、BATCH 均为
`h_normal` 轨迹。确认令牌按故障而非按场景。注入器可在任一适用场景叠加故障，
不适用的 `(sceneId, faultId)` 被拒绝。故障计划是评价者专用的
`(sceneId, faultId|h_normal)` 列表。

| ID | 属于 `H_single` | 依据 | 适用场景 | 机制 | 可观察签名 | 预期 T5／停机 | 令牌 |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| H-NORMAL | 否 | — | 全部八个已登记场景 | 无 IUT 叠加 | 无 `H_single` 叠加的场景轨迹 | FIND 主机应答落在 CRS-M1-00391 `[0,2]`（若发生 FIND） | H-NORMAL |
| F-FIND-ANSWER-LATE | 是 | CRS-M1-00391 | NORMAL、WAIT-CONTINUE、ABORT、SINGLE-BATCH | 2 s 之后、声明观测终点之前发出 FIND-HOST 应答 | FIND-ANSWER-LATE，时刻在 `(2, observationEnd)` | 相对 `[0,2]` 的 T5 FAIL；运行至观测终点 | FIND-ANSWER-LATE |
| F-FIND-ANSWER-ABSENT | 是 | CRS-M1-00391 | 同 LATE | 直至观测终点无 FIND-HOST 应答 | FIND-ANSWER-ABSENT；无主机应答事件 | 相对 `[0,2]` 的 T5 FAIL；在观测终点停机 | FIND-ANSWER-ABSENT |
| F-LUR-ACK-MISSING | 是 | CRS-M1-00366 | 同 LATE | 匹配 LUR WRQ 后 TH 省略 ACK | 有 LUR-WRQ、无 TH LUR-ACK | 无 TH LUR-ACK 时停机 | LUR-ACK-MISSING |

### 仅预实验的 LOCATE 偏离（不修改 `cltav_experiment_design.json`）

SC-SAME-KEY、SC-RESOURCE-ERROR、SC-INVALID-OBS 在本预实验中只用于 DETECT／ABLATION
计数，不用于 LOCATE。受控 CRS 下它们的域内假设少于 2 个（SC-INVALID-OBS 为评价者构造）。
SC-NO-RESPONSE 保留两个假设（`h_normal` 与 F-STATUS-SILENCE）；缺席路径仍按设计
NOT-SCORED。FIND 登记窗 `[0,3]` 属数据加载端，不作为 T5 判定区间发布；2 s 主机
应答保持 `[0,2]`（CRS-M1-00391）。

Truth-store 直接调用方仅限 `a615a_sim.evaluator` / `a615a_sim.faults`；测试前缀不再放行。
读写须持有由 `issue_truth_capability()` 签发的 `TruthCapability`；无令牌或伪造令牌失败关闭。
测试经评价者入口完成读写，不把真值当作算法输入。
