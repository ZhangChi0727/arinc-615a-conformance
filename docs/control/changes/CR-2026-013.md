## Why this change exists

Three consistent deliveries on one PR:

1. **Publication.** Centre the manuscript on ARINC 615A data-loading judgement and isolation under declared protocol, fault-domain, measurement and resource conditions. Keep CL-TAV as the method name. Tentative writing target: IEEE TAES Regular Paper; AST as an alternate. Institution CAS ranking remains `PENDING-INSTITUTIONAL-RANKING-CHECK`.
2. **645 binding.** Bind locally acquired ARINC 645-1 2021 only where 615A/665 already trigger integrity, check-value or naming obligations. Record SOURCE identity and SEMANTIC extraction as candidates. Keep CAPABILITY/EXECUTION `NOT-ESTABLISHED`.
3. **Algorithm and experiment specification.** Academic main algorithm with abstract sub-process contracts, plus a matching experiment architecture. No core solver selection, no confirmatory runs.

## Scope that is in

- Working title: “CL-TAV: Closed-Loop Test–Analysis Verification for ARINC 615A Data Loading under Timing Uncertainty” and the equivalent Chinese title.
- Two propositions to evaluate: (i) uncertainty-aware consistent observation interpretation, timing verdicts and compatibility update; (ii) feeding residual ambiguity into next-action selection with explicit resource/recovery/error/stop rules.
- 615A-triggered 645 CRC parameters, check-value enumeration, integrity-versus-security distinction, and 615A-relevant part-number/file-name rules.
- Top-level CL-TAV process and interface contracts IF-PRED-OBS, IF-HIST-UPDATE, IF-OBS-INTERPRET, IF-SELECT-ADMIT, IF-EXECUTE-RECORD, IF-PREP-RECOVER, IF-EQUIV, IF-RESOURCE-STOP.
- Experiment groups EXP-CLTAV-DETECT / LOCATE / ABLATION, four arms, truth-independence and fairness contracts, successor dependency table.
- N25-01: restore the Recover `Stop-Empty` assertion.

## Scope that is out

- Codec, protocol engine, executable verification engine, core estimators/solvers, network I/O, HIL, AFDX hardware, confirmatory experiments, tags, Configuration, M3.
- Claiming first invention of set intersection, interval verdicts or minimax.
- Binding 645 security/PDL chapters that 615A does not trigger.
- Auto-assigning new 645 rows to bound M2 transitions or expanding FIND/DOWNLOAD/AFDX execution.
- Setting any of CRC-VALIDATION, CHECK-VALUE-VALIDATION, NAMING-ALGORITHM-VALIDATION, COMPLETE-INTEGRITY-VALIDATION to ESTABLISHED.
